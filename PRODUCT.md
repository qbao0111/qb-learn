# Product

## Register

product

## Users

QB Learn is a personal study tool for its owner, used to review flashcards, practise questions, and manage imported question banks across desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Keep self-study fast and focused by making question-bank review, practice, testing, and backup available in one dependable interface.

## Brand Personality

Focused, tidy, and youthful. The interface should feel friendly without becoming distracting or decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud gamification, dense enterprise dashboards, novelty controls, and decorative effects that compete with study content.

## Design Principles

- Keep study content as the strongest visual priority.
- Prefer familiar controls that disappear into the workflow.
- Preserve user state and make preferences predictable across sessions.
- Use restrained color to communicate actions and learning outcomes.
- Make desktop and mobile workflows equally complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, large mobile touch targets, and a dark theme suitable for low-light study.
